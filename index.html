A reservoir of black liquid in the shape of a MacBook camera notch. It rises into peaks in time with music and leans toward the pointer.

A free, native macOS player that turns the MacBook notch into playback controls and black ferrofluid driven by system audio. Works with Apple Music and Spotify. Audio stays on your Mac.

The real player, filmed open: black liquid moves with the music while a track is skipped, the seek strip is scrubbed, and two-finger swipes move backward and forward through the queue.
